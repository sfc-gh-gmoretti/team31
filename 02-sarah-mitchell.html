Sarah Mitchell

Chief Risk Officer

Meeting objective

Establish the cost-of-inaction outcome and win Sarah as an active sponsor, not just a budget approver. She holds an approved remediation budget she can't spend and a self-service pilot she had to pause — recover both as intelligence.

Credibility opener — say first

“The FCA formal notice and the BCBS 239 finding land on your desk, and our understanding is you have remediation budget approved but stuck. We'd like to understand what's really blocking it and what 'compliant by Q4' has to look like — then show you where a governed platform actually moves that, rather than pitch you features.”
