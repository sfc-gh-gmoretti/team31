CTechnology · the coexistence gate

Marcus Okafor

Chief Technology Officer

Meeting objective

Neutralise the incumbent objection and frame coexistence, not rip-and-replace. Nothing proceeds without his endorsement. Understand his Microsoft politics, his security bar, and the sequencing that lets Charlotte publish on time.

Credibility opener — say first

“You joined 18 months ago with first-hand platform-evaluation experience, you're managing a significant Azure estate, and you want to approve on documented, defensible grounds without a political fight. We're not here to argue you out of Microsoft — we want to show where Snowflake coexists with it and gives you a defensible basis to say yes.”

The one takeaway to land

Leave them believing
“Snowflake coexists with Azure, meets your security and resilience bar, and gives you a defensible, documented basis to approve.”

What to show: Security and governance posture, deployment on Azure, and a coexistence architecture that reduces sprawl rather than adding a silo.

Objections & responses

If they push back…Respond
Why add a platform to my Microsoft estate?It's not additive — it subsumes seven fragmented risk systems into one governed platform that runs on Azure, so you reduce sprawl and finally get single-source lineage.
This becomes a political fight with Microsoft.Coexistence avoids that: Azure stays where it earns its keep, Snowflake is the neutral governed source of truth over the estate. You approve on documented grounds, not a vendor war.

Exit criteria — don't leave without

  • What makes the decision defensible to him
  • The Azure coexistence boundary
  • Security/resilience non-negotiables
  • Sequencing that unblocks Charlotte
  • His evidence bar for endorsement

Out-of-role debrief question (the 5 min after)

“Did we defuse the Microsoft/incumbent tension credibly, or did we sound like we were dismissing his Azure investment?”
