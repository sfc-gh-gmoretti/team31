Persona discovery sheets — walk the floor with questions, not assumptions

The anchoring outcome

One outcome, everything maps to it
Trusted, auditable regulatory reporting in days not weeks — satisfying the FCA and standing up under Basel IV — without ripping out Azure. Split into two groups; three personas before lunch, three after; reconvene at lunch to stitch. Open every meeting by establishing credibility, run tight discovery (5–7 questions), show one capability that maps to the persona, land one takeaway, and play it back before you leave.

The sequence

Before lunch — James (joint, orient) · then split: Sarah (Group A) & Charlotte (Group B)
After lunch — split: Priya (Group A) & Marcus (Group B) · then David (single) · then SE-AFE consultation
Next day — the CFO pitch: Andrew Harper (Going High / Wide / Fast)
Adapt — if Sarah's sponsorship is shaky, elevate Marcus early; David is the safe skip if time slips.

Influence map (who moves the decision)

  • Charlotte writes the recommendation → Marcus approves it → Sarah funds it → Andrew decides. To validate with James.
  • Priya holds the CEO's two-quarter AI deadline; David is ground truth; James (Accenture) is the CFO unlock and co-presenter.

Bring to the SE-AFE consultation

One sharp question, not an open brief. Likely candidates once discovery lands: “Can we state calculation-level lineage satisfies the FCA's Principle 3 bar, or must we hedge it 'to validate'?” and “Is the funded-first-step MVP scoped tightly enough to be fundable from Sarah's existing budget?”
