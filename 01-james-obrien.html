APartner · orientation & co-seller

James O'Brien

Managing Director, UK Banking Practice · Accenture

Meeting objective

Get the real account and competitive picture, and recruit James to walk into the CFO meeting beside you. He sits outside the bank and sees the whole estate — this is reconnaissance and alliance, not a pitch. Remember: the CFO is the hardest persona to unlock, and partners already have access.

Credibility opener — say first

“Before we get into it — our read is that Aldwych's core problem isn't a tooling gap, it's that seven ring-fenced risk systems can't trace a capital figure back to source, and the FCA notice has made that unignorable. You've lived this account for twelve years and run comparable programmes at other ring-fenced banks. So we mostly want to learn from you today — where we're right, where we're naïve, and how you'd orient us.”

The one takeaway to land

Leave them believing
“Snowflake and Accenture arrive as one team, and we understand this is a lineage-and-trust problem, not a feature sale.”

What to show: If pressed for a point of view, assert (don't demo heavily): governed, calculation-level lineage across all seven estates in one platform that coexists with Azure — then hand the floor back.

  • A recommended sequence for the afternoon
